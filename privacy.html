Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what TitanChatX ("we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

The short version: Secret chats are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read them. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Account details: email or phone number, username, display name, profile photoTo create your account, log you in and show you to friends
Messages, photos, voice notes, stickers, videos you send in normal chatsTo deliver and store your conversations
Secret chats: stored only as encrypted text. Your private key stays on your deviceSo only you and your friend can read them
Online status and last seenTo show presence. You can hide last seen in Settings
Game data: scores, duels, Titan Coins, purchases of themes and bubble styles, story endingsTo run games, rewards and the coin shop
Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)To notify you of messages and calls
Call connection data, including IP addressTo set up voice and video calls
Optional encrypted key backupTo restore secret chats on a new phone. We cannot read it without your recovery code
Basic technical and crash informationTo keep the app working and secure

2. Titan AI

When you use Titan AI, the text needed for that request (for example the messages you ask it to summarize) is sent to a third-party AI provider (Groq or Google Gemini) to produce the answer. Secret chats are never sent to Titan AI, search, Spaces or Recall. You can switch Titan AI off in Settings, Privacy and notifications.

3. Calls

Calls use WebRTC and are sent directly between devices when possible. When a direct link is not possible, they pass through a relay server (TURN, provided by Metered) that carries encrypted media and sees your IP address, but not the content of the call.

4. Who we share data with

We use service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), Google Firebase (push notifications), our AI providers (see section 2), and Metered (call relay). We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your profile, messages, media, coins and game data, except where we must keep something to meet a legal duty or stop abuse. Backups are removed within a short period afterwards.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Hide your last seen, block people, and control notification previews in Settings.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data.
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account.

To use these rights, email teamnirux@gmail.com. If you are in India, you may also have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Secret chats use end-to-end encryption. No system is perfectly secure, and if you lose your device and your recovery code, secret chats cannot be restored.

8. Children

TitanChatX is not for children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). If we learn a child under that age has an account, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
