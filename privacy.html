Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what TitanChatX collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We try to collect only what the app needs to work.

Who we are

TitanChatX is run by TitanChatX, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. For anything about your data, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL. Grievance officer: Devasena K.

Who can use TitanChatX

TitanChatX is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. If you believe a child is using the app, please tell us at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Email address and a password (a phone number too, if phone login is offered)To create your account, log you in and recover your account
Username, display name, photo, bioSo friends can find you and know who you are
Messages, photos, videos, voice messages, stories and other things you postTo deliver them to the people you choose
Online status and last seenTo show friends when you are online. You can turn this off in Settings, then Privacy

We do not ask for your contacts, location or microphone and camera unless you use a feature that needs them, such as a call or a voice message. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Messages and encryption

Secret chats are end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read them. Normal chats are stored on our servers so that search and other features can work. Please do not share anything in a normal chat that you would want to keep from us.

Who we share data with

Only with the services that help us run TitanChatX, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase stores accounts, messages, files and live activity.
  • Cloudflare serves our website.
  • Resend sends sign-up and password reset emails.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications to your phone.
  • Groq processes the text of a chat only when you ask Titan AI to work on it. It never works on secret chats.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts shown in the app and on this website.
  • YouTube plays videos in Watch Together. YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply to the video player.

We also share data when the law requires it, or to protect people from serious harm. Other people on TitanChatX see what you choose to share with them, such as your name, photo, status and anything you post.

Where data is kept and for how long

  • Your account and content stay until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Stories and some updates expire on their own after a set time.
  • When you delete a message for everyone, it is kept for 30 days so you can restore it, then removed.
  • When you delete your account, your profile and content are removed from our database. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire.
  • Reports about abuse are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and to protect users.

Our service providers store data on servers that may be outside India.

Your choices and rights

  • Privacy settings. Hide your online status, choose who can visit your space, pause room invitations, block and report people.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to You, then Delete my account. You can also ask us on our delete account page.
  • Access and correction. You can see and edit your profile in the app. To ask for a copy of your data or to fix something you cannot edit, write to us.
  • Withdraw consent. You can stop using TitanChatX at any time. Deleting your account withdraws your consent for the data it holds.
  • Complain. Write to our grievance officer above. You may also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules in our database, and limits on what the app can do. No service is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password and do not share your login codes.

Emails

We send emails only to run your account, such as confirming your sign-up or resetting your password. We do not send marketing emails. If that ever changes, we will ask you first and every email will have an unsubscribe link.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change starts. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
