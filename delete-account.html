Delete your account

Last updated: 23 November 2026

You can delete your TitanChatX account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TitanChatX and go to the You tab.
  2. Choose Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you cannot open the app

Send an email to teamnirux@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my TitanChatX account". Write from the email address registered to your account, or include your username and the phone number you log in with, so we can check it is you. We will delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photo, bio, username and settings.
  • Your messages, stories, Drops, capsules, avatar, space, XP and game history.
  • Your login details.

What may remain

  • Messages you sent stay in other people's chats only if the app keeps a copy for them. Ask us if you need help with this.
  • Reports about abuse may be kept for as long as needed to deal with them and to protect other users, or where the law requires it.
  • Copies in backups are removed when the backups expire.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. To ask for a copy of your data first, write to teamnirux@gmail.com.
