Delete your TitanChatX account

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email teamnirux@gmail.com from the email address (or with the phone number) you use for TitanChatX.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my TitanChatX account" and include your username.
  3. We confirm it is you, then delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, photos, messages and media you sent, Titan Coins, purchases, game data, push token and encrypted key backup.

What may be kept

Limited records we must keep by law or to prevent abuse, for a limited time. Messages you sent may still appear on your friends' devices.

Deleting your account is permanent. Titan Coins and purchased themes cannot be restored.
