PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Clear about what is private, and what is not.

We would rather tell you exactly how it works than make big promises.

End-to-end encrypted secret chats

Text and photos in a secret chat are encrypted on your device. We cannot read them.

AI only when you ask

Titan AI sends nothing until you tap a button, and it never touches secret chats.

You control what others see

Hide last seen, block people, and turn off message previews in notifications.

Delete your account in the app

One place in Settings removes your account and data. Details in our privacy policy.

The honest details

Good privacy is knowing the limits.

  • Only secret chats are end-to-end encrypted. Normal chats are stored on our servers so features like search and Titan AI can work.
  • Secret chats are tied to one phone. A recovery code lets you restore them on a new phone. If you lose both, they cannot be recovered.
  • When you use Titan AI on a normal chat, the text for that request is processed by an AI provider.
  • We do not sell your data.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is every chat end-to-end encrypted?

No. Secret chats are end-to-end encrypted. Normal chats are stored on our servers so search, Titan AI and other features can work. You choose which kind of chat to use.

Can I get back messages I deleted for everyone?

Yes, your own. Recall keeps messages you delete for everyone for 30 days so you can restore them. Other people still see "Message deleted" unless you allow them to see it.

Does Titan AI read all my chats?

No. It only reads the recent messages of a chat when you tap one of its buttons, and it never works on secret chats. Daily use is limited.

Which phones does it work on?

Android first. We have no date to share for other platforms yet.

Do calls work between countries?

Calls try to connect directly and use a relay server when your networks block that, which helps across countries and on mobile data. Quality still depends on your internet.

What are Titan Coins?

An in-app reward earned from daily bonuses and games. You can spend them on chat themes and bubble styles. They have no cash value.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and choose Delete my account, or follow the steps on our delete account page.
