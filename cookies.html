Cookie Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Short version: this website does not use cookies, and it does not use advertising or tracking tools.

What we use

titanchatx.questtitan.in does not set cookies and does not store information in your browser to track you. We do not run analytics, advertising or social media trackers on this site. Because of that, you will not see a cookie banner here.

Fonts

This site loads its font from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser asks Google for the font file, so Google can see your IP address and browser details. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Our hosting

Our website is delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web host, it keeps basic technical logs, such as your IP address and the time of the request, to keep the site secure and working.

The TitanChatX app

The app does not use cookies. It stores your login on your device so you stay signed in. Our Privacy Policy explains the data the app handles.

If this changes

If we add analytics or any other tool that uses cookies, we will update this page and ask for your consent with a banner before the tool runs.
